HR Leave and Attendance Policy - Version 3.2

1. Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly at a rate of 2 days per month. Unused leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year; any balance beyond that is forfeited unless the employee's manager approves an exception in writing.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for any sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days. Sick leave does not carry forward and resets each January 1st.

3. Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 8 days per month, subject to manager approval and role eligibility. Engineering, Data, and Design roles are generally eligible for hybrid schedules; roles requiring on-site presence (Facilities, Front Desk, Lab Operations) are not eligible unless approved by HR leadership.

4. Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are entitled to 26 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers are entitled to 4 weeks of paid leave. Parental leave requests must be submitted at least 30 days in advance where possible.

5. Notice Period
Standard notice period for resignation is 60 days for employees at Senior Associate level and above, and 30 days for all other levels. The company may, at its discretion, offer a buyout of the notice period.

6. Reimbursement of Unused Leave
Upon separation from the company, employees are reimbursed for unused annual leave (up to the carry-forward cap) at their current per-day salary rate. Sick leave is not reimbursed.

7. Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year as published in the annual HR calendar. Employees required to work on a public holiday are entitled to a compensatory day off within 30 days.
